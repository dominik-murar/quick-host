5 "My location" landed on the train While following a ride the map's selected vehicle is the tracked one, so the locate request frames you and the vehicle, and following re-frames it.

  • Map highlight = the leg page open in the journey drawer, else the tracked leg on its overview.
  • Drawer reopens on the tracked leg while its vehicle has left your boarding stop, else on the journey overview.

PRs

Independent from main: 1, 2, 3. Stacked: 4 → 5 → 6 → 7 (they share the on-board model in ride.ts).

1. fix(rail): report points trains actually use — patch

Research: mapa.zsr.sk has no catalogue of dopravné body, only an undocumented live-trains call (api/action gtall, session cookie, no licence). ŽSR names the loop Výh. Ružín; live it also reports at Výh. Drienovec, Dúbrava, Lučivná, Nivy, Paludza, Potvorice, Výhybňa Váh and several odbočky that OSM lacks. 11 of 83 sampled live trains sat > 300 m from any of our points. ŽSR does list passages at Kysak výh. č. 35/36, so switches stay.

  • The timetable estimate stops short only of the next point the trip stops at, not of points it passes (switches, loops, junctions). Fixes Kysak.
  • Add the missing ŽSR report points as a curated list in build-rail-network.mjs (names from ŽSR, coordinates from where Ubian reports trains there), merged into the OSM points. No build-time dependency on mapa.zsr.sk.
  • A small scripts/find-missing-report-points.mjs samples live trains and lists positions far from any point, for refreshing that list.

2. feat(map): rail network at every zoom — minor

  • Draw our own RAIL_NETWORK tracks as one quiet line layer under routes, visible from country zoom.

3. fix(map): my location shows you — patch

  • Locate frames only you and pauses following the vehicle until you open the ride again or tap the vehicle.
  • On board a train the camera frames you and the next station, so it zooms out between stations and in as you arrive.

4. fix(ride): your position wins on board a train — patch

  • Trains: on-board if within 250 m of the track (GPS in a carriage), 500 m once aboard. Buses keep 50 m.
  • On board: your dot snaps to the line and carries the vehicle; no reach arrow, hatch or pulse; next stop, passed stops and the timeline come from your position, not the timetable guess.

5. feat(ride): boarding confidence — minor

  • High (today's checks, train tolerances from #4): join automatically.
  • Medium: you move along the ride's line past your boarding stop at vehicle speed (> 300 m along the line, > 15 km/h), around when the delayed timetable has it there, while the vehicle reports before your stop, too far behind, or not at all → a floating Som vo vozidle pill on your dot.
  • Confirmed, you ride on your position even with no vehicle report; Nie som vo vozidle or leaving the line for 2 min undoes it.

6. fix(journey): hand over legs on time — patch

New hand-over rules, first match wins:

  1. vehicle reported past the exit (as today);
  2. you are on board the next leg's vehicle (high confidence);
  3. you are past the exit on foot: off the line > 150 m within 300 m of the exit, or the exit's delayed arrival has passed and the vehicle stopped reporting for 2 min;
  4. timetable exit + 60 min (as today).
  • When several vehicles share a trip, take the one that fits: nearest you on the line, else the smallest delay.

7. feat(ride): say when data is stale — minor

  • No successful vehicle update for 45 s (or the browser is offline): the ride drawer says Bez spojenia · údaje spred N min.
  • On board, the ride keeps moving by your position: passed stops from GPS, times shifted by the delay estimated from your progress against the timetable.
