The journey drawer: planner and Jazda as one

Spoj · proposal with mockups · 2026-10-28 · rev 2: decisions folded in, list vs card comparison

One journey surface. A journey opened from the planner, from departures or from the map opens in its own journey drawer, stacked over the main drawer. It looks the same whether you track it or not; tracking only adds live state (headline, progress bar, vehicle on the rail).

The pill is that drawer, tucked. Minimise a tracked journey and the drawer shrinks into the pill above the main drawer. Tap the pill and it grows back into the same drawer you planned with.

Two tiers inside it. Tier 1 is the journey (steps). Tier 2 is one leg (every stop, earlier and later trips, departures after you arrive at a stop). Tier 3, optional, is one stop's full departures. Back pops these, then closes the drawer.

Same CTAs everywhere: Sledovať / Nesledovať plus the ticket button, under the headline on every tier, so a ticket is one tap away at any point of a journey. Jazda's separate stop button goes away.

A single-trip connection (a departure row, a vehicle tapped on the map) opens straight at tier 2 as its root, since it has no steps to list.

Open: tap a journey card, a departure or a vehicle → the journey drawer slides up at medium; the map frames the journey.
Leg: tap a leg's title row (›) → tier 2 slides in from the right; the map frames that leg.
Stop: in tier 2 tap a stop → departures after you arrive unfold inline; Všetky odchody › → tier 3.
Track: Sledovať → same drawer, live. Header button turns from ✕ into ⌄ (minimise).
Tuck: ⌄, or drag a tracked journey below peek → it shrinks into the pill and the main drawer rises back.
Untuck: tap or drag up the pill → it grows into the journey drawer at medium: at tier 2 of the leg you ride, or at tier 1 while you wait or walk (before boarding, between legs).
Back (Android key, Android browser back, iOS edge swipe): tier 3 → 2 → 1, then closes the drawer.
Closing a tracked journey tucks it into the pill, as back from Jazda does today. Closing an untracked one dismisses it. The one exception stays as now: leaving the app with an untracked journey open that departs within 30 min starts tracking it.
Nesledovať leaves you in the open drawer, untracked; ✕ then dismisses it.

What changes in code

AreaChange
StateScreen loses mode: 'details', detailsBackMode and selectedConnection. A new JourneyView holds { journey, pages: ('leg' | 'stop')[], tucked }. The tracked ride still names its journey by uid, so the pill is simply a tucked JourneyView whose journey is tracked.
DrawersA second sheet, JourneyDrawer, with its own sheetHeight, stages and swipe-back, reusing useSheetResponder and useSwipeBack. Its expanded stage is the full height. The main drawer's rideMorph fade-out goes away.
Tier 1JourneyOverview merges JourneyDetails with the top of RideStops (ridden legs, current leg). JourneySteps stays and loses in-place unfolding: a tap opens tier 2. Swiping a leg for another trip stays.
Tier 2LegView merges ConnectionDetails and Jazda's timeline: TripTimeline plus ArrivalDepartures plus alternative chips from useLegAlternatives. A single-trip connection uses it as its root.
Tier 3StopView reuses StopCard and DepartureRow with useArrivalDepartures. OpenStopDepartures pushes this page instead of jumping drawers.
HeadlineA single journeyHeadline(journey, ride, vehicles) returns { title, countdown, progress | null }: departureHeadline when not tracked, rideInstruction, rideCountdown and rideProgress when tracked. The pill and the drawer header both read it, which is what lets one morph into the other.
CTAsJourneyActions is the only place for Sledovať/Nesledovať and the ticket. Jazda's stop SheetButton is deleted.
PillLiveRide shrinks to the strip, plus a morph into the JourneyDrawer header (badge, title, countdown, bar), using the same interpolations as today.
BackdrawerBackSteps gains 'journey' and one step per page. Android's hardware back and the Android browser's history (useBackHandler) pop them. At the root, back tucks a tracked journey and closes an untracked one. The iOS edge swipe pops pages inside the journey drawer only.
MapbottomInset, routes and boarding stop come from the journey drawer while it is open. Tier 2 frames its leg. A tucked journey, or one at peek, keeps the map following its vehicle as today.
DesktopThe journey drawer takes over the sidebar, with back in its header. There is still no tracking, so no pill.
DeletedConnectionDetails, RideStops, rideOpen/stageBeforeRide, the stripShown special case, toggleLeg/selectLeg unfolding, and the details branch of previousScreen.

Suggested PRs: (1) the journey drawer and navigation, keeping today's content; (2) the unified headline, CTAs and the pill morph; (3) tier 2 and tier 3 pages. Each PR stays shippable.

Decisions

  1. Untracked journeys don't tuck. ✕ dismisses them. Exception kept from #92: leaving the app with a journey open that departs within 30 min tracks it, so it comes back as the pill.
  2. The pill reopens at the active leg's tier 2 while you ride, and at tier 1 while you wait or walk. The back stack is built as if you had navigated there, so back from 7 goes to tier 1, then tucks.
  3. Alternatives in tier 2 as one-line chips, plus the existing swipe on tier 1 rows. Chips hide on a leg you are riding. The CTAs sit above them on every tier, so the ticket stays reachable throughout.
  4. The main drawer is hidden while a journey is open.
  5. Open: card or plain list for the current leg in tier 1, see the comparison above. Recommended: plain list.
