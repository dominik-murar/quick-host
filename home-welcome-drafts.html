The welcome should adapt, not occupy the journey.

Once the page starts at the current module, a large generic “Vitaj!” no longer earns its space. Here are three ways to resolve it without making completed lessons feel hidden.

✦
Recommendation: A — progressive welcome

Keep the expressive welcome for a learner’s first visit only. For returning learners, pin the useful controls and replace the greeting with a small upward-scroll cue.

Greeting with a job

“Vitaj!” stays, but gains context and points to the resumed module.

It is the clearest narrative, though it reintroduces vertical chrome we were trying to remove.

Recommended behavior

No progress: start at the top and show the full “Vitaj!” onboarding moment.
Returning learner: align the current module below the pinned utility bar and show the upward cue.
Scrolling up: reveal completed modules naturally; keep them interactive and remove the cue once the first completed card is visible.
