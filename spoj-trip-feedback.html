Trip feedback, prioritised

Feedback from the last trip, grouped into 13 items and ranked by impact on the core flow (find a connection, then ride it) and on the honest-data principle, against effort in the current codebase. Effort: S under a day, M a few days, L a week or more, XL needs a data source we don't have yet.

P0Now: bugs and cheap trust wins

Small fixes for things that broke or misled you during the trip. Ship these as one batch.

1Train in station covers the station touch targetS · bug

The vehicle marker sits on top of the stop, so you can't tap the stop. Fix the hit order or offset in LiveMap.web.tsx. #6 changes the train marker anyway, so this fix may come out of that work.

2Make the stop highlight work for short Odchody cardsS · bug

The highlight on short cards never shows. focusedGroup (TransitScreen.tsx) only focuses a card that covers at least half of the visible list, which a stop card with a single departure never does. Even with that rule fixed, the last few cards can't scroll up to a focus line near the top.

  • A focus line that slides near the end: the focus line stays fixed near the top. Once the remaining scroll distance is less than one visible list height H, it moves down by the same amount you scroll (line = top + max(0, H − remaining)), so it reaches the bottom of the list at the end. Every card crosses the line once, in order, without extra padding or a longer scroll.
  • Tap to focus: tapping a stop heading focuses that stop. This covers lists too short to scroll.
  • Rejected: dynamic bottom padding. It adds up to a screen of empty space after short cards, and the scroll length changes whenever departures refresh.

3Persist the tracked journey across reloadS

Mobile browsers kill backgrounded PWAs all the time, so today you lose Jazda mid-ride. Store the tripID and selection in localStorage (the same pattern as search selections in useTransitData.ts) and restore it if the trip hasn't ended.

4Show town names consistently: always for regional bus stops and train stations, never for MHD or mixed stopsS

Search results already follow this rule (autocompleteStopName in domain/stops.ts). Nearby stops don't: Odchody card headings show no town at all ("žel.st.", "OcÚ"), and on the map train stations get the town but regional bus stops don't ("žel. stanica"). The fix is to apply the same naming rule, from one shared function, to nearby stops on the map, Odchody headings and Jazda.

5Location permission on load, with an explainerS–M

Show a short "what Spoj does" screen with a Share location button. Remember the first grant, and on later visits request it on load so the browser can re-prompt without the explainer. Without a grant, search falls back to the Odkiaľ-first flow in #7.

P1Next: the honest-data principle and journey planning

The highest product value. The first item is about trust and is cheap to start, so it leads.

6Trains: show "last reported", not "is here"S → L, in two steps

What Ubian gives us (checked against live trains around Košice). Report points aren't flagged, but they can be inferred. Reported positions snap exactly onto station coordinates and stay unchanged between jumps. trip_stops lists the stations a train passes without stopping (crossing: 1, with coordinates but no times): EN 442 has 83 stations, of which 15 are stops. That list isn't complete, because some trains sit on points that match no listed station, probably junctions. lastStopOrder points to the last stopping station, not the last report point. lastCommunication is always 0001-01-01, so there is no report time. Train route geometry is straight lines between stopping stations. We don't scrape mapa.zsr.sk, because its terms forbid automated use.

  • 6a, glyph (S–M): match the vehicle's coordinates to a station in its trip stop list, mark that station as the last report point and the next listed station as the next one, and label the train "reported at X". The age ("Y min ago") comes from when we saw the position change, so it's unknown until the first jump after load. When the position matches no listed station, fall back to "between X and Y".
  • 6b, track section (M–L): draw the track between the last and the next report point. This needs real track geometry from OpenStreetMap railways (ODbL), either from the base map's own rail layer or a precomputed extract. Ubian's own route data is only straight lines.
  • Countdown to arrival based on the user's location builds on 6a and can wait.

10Zoom-out: trains and long-distance only, stops into city targetsM–L

Today, when you're zoomed out beyond what one request covers, the map is intentionally empty. Instead, request only trains and long-distance or regional vehicles. Don't bundle stops: collapse them into one target per city, where a tap shows the main train and regional bus hubs, or zooms in. This depends on #4 for city grouping and may need different upstream filters (cityID, radius).

11Saved placesL

  • Basic saved places first (name and location). These also seed the Odkiaľ/Kam suggestions in #7.
  • Annotating direct connections in Odchody needs the stop list for each departure, which means N extra trip_stops calls. Cache them per trip and limit them to visible cards.
  • Per-place stop radius and a stop allow/deny list come last. This is power-user tuning.

P3Research first: offline

12Offline access: local stops, timetables, approximate connectionsXL

The service worker only caches the app shell, and every endpoint we use is live and undocumented, with no bulk export. Doing this properly needs a timetable source (GTFS or CIS JŘ exports) plus local routing, which makes it a project of its own. Start with a spike on data availability. A cheap interim step is caching the last stops and departures responses for saved places.
