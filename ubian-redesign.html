Návrh redizajnu · Ubian

All of Slovakia's transport.
One calm app.

Ubian already does what no other Slovak app does: timetables and live positions for trains, regional buses and city transport in about 55 cities, plus credit, transport cards, tickets, student passes, parking and bikesharing. This proposal keeps all of that and rebuilds the experience around the few seconds people actually spend in a transit app.

An unsolicited concept, not affiliated with Ubian. The palette comes from the current Ubian app: green, navy, and the orange / blue / red mode icons. The wordmark is a stand-in for Ubian's logo. The patterns come from my IDS Východ design-system work.

01 · Where Ubian stands

A strong engine behind a dated interface

Based on the iOS app, ubian.sk and its live-data endpoints, as observed on 3 Oct 2026. I haven't reviewed store ratings or support data.

Strengths

  • Unique coverage: city transport in about 55 cities, regional buses and trains in one place.
  • Live data already exists: vehicle positions, delays, cancellations, current stop, low-floor, Wi-Fi and bike flags.
  • A full commerce stack: credit, chip and virtual transport card, time tickets, single tickets, student passes, parking, Google Pay.
  • Already innovating: an AI agent (beta) for journey search, and bikesharing on the map.
  • Operators already trust it: DPMK's own live departures run on Ubian.

Friction

  • The city is a manual choice from a list of about 55 "MHD <city>" entries, when the phone already knows where you are.
  • Transport type is one of 7 combinations ("Vlaky / Autobusy / MHD", "Vlaky / MHD"…). Three toggles do the same job.
  • Commerce is split into separate silos: e-shop, basket, wallet, card, transfer of card products, student pass. Users have to know Ubian's internal model to buy something.
  • Train delays are a separate page instead of being part of every journey.
  • The AI agent is a separate chat instead of part of search.
  • The web is built on Bootstrap and jQuery UI with icon-font glyphs, so it feels like a website rather than a native app.

In the iOS app today

  • Five tabs organised by product: Connections · Card · Ticket · Parking · My Ubian. Three of the five are commerce, while departures hide behind a toggle inside Connections.
  • The map is a separate pushed screen with Stops / Vehicle position / Bikes toggles. The live data, Ubian's strongest asset, is two taps away.
  • The Search button looks disabled (pale green) until a destination is chosen, and the planner then needs that extra tap.
  • The home screen mixes search, the active ticket, history, favourites and four commerce tiles, so there's no single focus.
  • Solid green bars and centred titles predate today's iOS style: no large titles, no Liquid Glass, no bottom accessory.

Worth keeping

  • The active-ticket card with a validity progress bar ("Ticket is valid for 356 days"). It carries over into Peňaženka.
  • The quick "No transfers" toggle, which becomes a planner chip.
  • The mode coding: orange trains, blue buses, red city transport. It's recognisable, so the redesign keeps it.
  • Current position as the default origin, and the ⇅ swap.
!
Contrast
White on Ubian green #33B45D is only 2.7:1, and the mode icons are 2.1–3.3:1. The header titles and the Search label fail WCAG AA.
02 · Principles

What the redesign optimises for

⏱ Seconds, not screens

Your departures with 0 taps, a route in 2, a ticket shown in 1. Every flow is measured in taps.

📍 Context instead of configuration

The city, operator and tariff follow your location and route. Nobody should pick "MHD Košice" from a list again.

🧭 Native, not generic

Liquid Glass and SF on iOS, Material 3 Expressive on Android. Ubian's identity lives in the content: lines, rides and cards.

👛 One wallet

Credit, cards, tickets, passes and parking in one place, grouped by "what's valid now" rather than by product type.

📶 Honest data

A status ("načas" or "+2 min") appears only when the data is live, and nothing else is needed to say so. Offline, you get saved places and tickets, never a blank screen.

♿ Everyone rides

WCAG 2.2 AA, Dynamic Type / 200 %, step-free routing, and low-floor flags (already in the data).

03 · Structure

One map. One sheet. One wallet button.

Live vehicle positions are Ubian's most valuable data, yet today the map is two taps away. In the redesign the map is the app: a live map that never leaves the screen, with everything else layered on it as a sheet. There are no tabs, so there's no tab switching.

Canvas

Live map

Vehicles, your stops and places, bikesharing. The camera follows whatever the sheet shows.

Default

Odchody in the sheet

The nearest stops first, plus any stops you pin. The map shows the vehicles for those departures, approaching for real.

On demand

Search on the sheet's edge

"Kam a kedy?" rides on the top edge of the sheet, like Apple Maps. At the default medium height that's the lower half of the screen. Tapping it expands the sheet into the planner.

Always 1 tap

🎫 Peňaženka

A dedicated button next to search opens a full-screen wallet. It's predictable, so nobody panics at a ticket check.

The sheet

  • Sheet header, always visible: [🔍 Kam a kedy? 🎙] [🎫] [👤], sitting on the sheet's top edge. Its position follows the sheet: low when collapsed, around mid-screen at the default. A green dot on 🎫 means something is valid right now. 👤 opens account, settings, parking history and support (what used to be "Ďalšie").
  • Three heights, as in Apple Maps: collapsed (a floating glass capsule with just the header, for the full map), medium, the default (Kam: destination shortcuts with live travel times; then Odchody from the nearest stops), large (the full list or the planner).
  • The content follows the job: Odchody → planner → results → Detail spoja → Jazda. A departure row opens Detail spoja directly. Back or swiping down returns one level. Map controls (map style, my location) float just above the sheet.
  • Live trip strip: once you tap "Sledovať", the ride is tracked until you end it. The strip floats just above the sheet in every state (medium, collapsed, results) and moves with it, and Peňaženka shows a compact ride line at its top. The collapsed map keeps following your vehicle. Tap the strip and the sheet becomes Jazda. Tap it and the sheet becomes Jazda, with the map following your vehicle.

Native on both platforms

  • iOS 26: MapKit with a persistent .sheet using presentationDetents([.height(86), .medium, .large]) and .medium selected by default and presentationBackgroundInteraction(.enabled), so the map stays interactive behind it. Liquid Glass sheet; Peňaženka is a .fullScreenCover. This is the Apple Maps / Find My model.
  • Android: MapLibre with BottomSheetScaffold (peek height, drag handle), the M3 SearchBar in the sheet (avatar inside, like Google Maps), and Peňaženka as a full-screen dialog. Predictive back collapses the sheet one level at a time.
  • Nastavenia holds the defaults: the transport modes and the start view. Default view is just the default sheet state: Odchody (medium), Mapa (peek) or Hľadať (focused field).

04 · Foundations

Ubian green, made accessible

The palette keeps Ubian's own colours and fixes their contrast. The brand green stays for surfaces and identity, with navy text on it (5.6:1). Interactive text and white-on-colour use darker shades of the same hues. In dark mode, the original bright colours come back: they reach 5.3–8.3:1 against dark surfaces.

Operator colours, made safe automatically

Where an operator publishes its own line colours (e.g. a city with numbered colour-coded lines), they replace the mode colour. The design system keeps the hue and adjusts lightness until white text reaches 4.5:1. The shape always encodes the mode, whatever colour an operator chose.

When an operator supplies no colour, Ubian's mode coding above applies. The mode is always shown three ways: colour, shape and glyph, so riders who can't tell colours apart and anyone in bright sunlight can still read it.

Type and motion

System fonts

SF Pro on iOS, Roboto Flex on Android and the system UI font on the web. They give native feel, Dynamic Type and full Slovak diacritics for free. Times and prices use tabular figures. There's no custom typeface unless Ubian's brand requires one.

Motion that explains

The search morph, routes drawing themselves, vehicles gliding between GPS updates, departures collapsing away, a pulse at your destination. Everything respects Reduce Motion, and text never moves while you read it.

One format for every row

  • Line · destination · platform. The platform is a light letter or number after the name, as CP shows it ("A", "C", track "3").
  • Live position as words on the second line: "na zastávke Letná · 4 zast.", from lastStopOrder and isOnStop. Together with the map, you can see a "1 min" tram is still two stops out.
  • Time + status on the right: "načas" or "+2 min". No live data: just the time, with no second line and no status.
  • Tapping a row opens Detail spoja, with that vehicle highlighted on the map, its path to the stop and your walk there.

Why fold the AI into search

  • The same field takes "Hlavná", "Domov" or a whole sentence. The model's job is to fill in the form, shown as editable chips, not to chat.
  • The chips make the interpretation visible and correctable in one tap, so nothing is a black box.
  • Three toggles (Vlaky · Autobusy · MHD) inside Filtre replace the 7 transport combinations. The chip shows how many filters are active.
  • No personal data goes to the model. The parsed query goes to Ubian's search engine.

Three actions: show, track, buy

  • Tap selects. A result is a choice among alternatives, so the first tap highlights it: its route draws on the map and the card opens a glance (legs, operators) with the two actions. Tapping again deselects it, and selecting another moves the highlight. Only one card is selected at a time.
  • › at the card's right edge (a 44 pt target) opens Detail spoja, the same view a departure row opens: line and direction, status, boarding stop, the stops to your exit, and the vehicle on the map.
  • Detail repeats the actions: Sledovať and Kúpiť sit at its bottom, so you can decide after looking without going back.
  • Kúpiť carries the context: from Výsledky it buys the whole journey, from Odchody a single ticket for that line.

Built on data Ubian already serves

The live endpoint returns delayMinutes, lastStopOrder, isOnStop, canceled, lowFloor, wifi, bicycle, the operator, and coordinates. That's everything this strip, the Jazda timeline and the Live Activity need, so no new backend is required to start.

Always with you: while a ride is tracked, a compact strip (line, "Vystúpte o 3 zast.", minutes, mini track) floats above the sheet in every state, and as a line at the top of Peňaženka. Tracking a ride and holding a ticket stay separate, but each links to the other.

One wallet, sorted by validity

  • Valid now (keeping today's validity progress bar): the virtual card with an active product, a single ticket, a running parking session. It shows a live band, the seconds clock and a rotating QR.
  • Credit: one balance with "Dobiť" (Google Pay / Apple Pay / card).
  • Cards and passes: chip card (and transferring its products), virtual card, student or teacher pass.
  • Buying starts from context: from a route ("Kúpiť lístok na túto cestu"), from a card ("Predĺžiť"), or from a parking zone.

Destinations, not starting points

The starting point is almost always "where I am", so each shortcut answers one question: how long to get there from here (door to door, live). One tap gives the results. The place you're at shows "Tu ste". Departures are their own section, so the two never mix. A destination can be in another city (live in Prešov, work in Košice).

07 · Data platform

Ubian is already a national data hub. Make it official.

What I found

  • Slovakia's national answer is CYRIL, the state app, but the official EU access-point entry (Delegated Regulation 2017/1926) is still a ŽSR track-closure map, and I found no open, documented real-time feed for developers.
  • Only a few feeds are open: ŽSR trains (GTFS, CC0) and Prešov city transport (GTFS, CC BY 4.0). Košice publishes only an expired JDF export.
  • Ubian's site gets live vehicles from an undocumented JSON endpoint that answers plain requests without authentication and sends no cross-origin permission header. It works, but it's not a product: there's no documentation, licence or terms.

Proposal: Ubian Open Data

  • Publish GTFS + GTFS-RT (vehicle positions, trip updates, service alerts) for operators that agree. Ubian becomes the de facto national access point.
  • A documented API with keys, rate limits, a CORS policy and clear terms, free for non-commercial use and paid for B2B.
  • Put the internal endpoints behind the same gateway: caching and rate limiting protect the live service.
  • That's a ready answer for tenders like IDS Východ's, which require live delays and positions from every operator.

Open questions

  • Ubian's brand guidelines: the palette and wordmark here are placeholders.
  • Which operators allow Ubian to republish their data, and under what licence?
  • App store reviews and support tickets, to rank the friction points with evidence.
  • Could the virtual card become an Apple Wallet / Google Wallet pass, or does validation require the rotating in-app QR?
