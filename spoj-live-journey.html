Where does the live journey live?

The music player's mini-player feels right because it has a fixed dock above the tab bar, and the full player is a modal over everything. Spoj's pill has neither. It rides the drawer's top edge, which moves, and it is a copy of the journey's own header living outside the drawer. So it reads as a third surface wedged between the map and the drawer, not as the drawer's "now playing". Below are four ways to give it a home, and what a native build adds.

A · A live chip in the drawer header recommended

The wallet button turns into a chip showing the line and the countdown, with progress along its bottom edge. It is the same slot the planned-trips plan already uses: wallet, then the next planned trip, then the live ride. Going back slides the journey page away, and its headline shrinks into the chip of the page underneath, the way an iOS title shrinks into the back button. Tapping the chip grows it back into the headline. It lives inside the drawer, so it is visible at every height, never floats, and never moves the controls.

CostsIt is less glanceable than the pill: the line and countdown show, but not "Vystúp na Hodžovo nám.". A small subtitle (vystúp, nastúp, prestup, choď) covers the verb.

B · Ride mode: the journey becomes the drawer's root

This is how navigation apps work. While a journey is tracked, the stack flips: the journey is the bottom page and home is a page you push over it with 🔍. Back from home returns to the ride, so nothing ever has to split off. Every page over the ride gets a live back button. The only way out is Ukončiť jazdu, or the ride ending by itself after the exit stop.

Native · The lock screen, the Dynamic Island and Android Live Updates worth it

This is where the "now playing" feeling actually comes from. The OS gives a persistent surface with a fixed home, which is exactly what the in-app pill lacks. A PWA can't do any of this: the closest it gets is a Web Push "Vystupuješ na ďalšej", which needs a server.

The countdown ticks on its own (Text(timerInterval:)). Updates are only needed on a step change or a delay.

Suggestion

  • Now (PWA): A. Make the live ride the third state of the wallet slot (wallet, then planned trip, then live). The journey page's headline shrinks into the chip on back and grows out of it on tap. The pill, its tuck and its merge strip go away.
  • Borrow one thing from B: opening the app while a ride is under way lands on the journey page with home underneath. That gives nav-mode focus without the trap.
  • Native: build the Live Activity and the Android Live Update first. This is the real now-playing pill, and "Vystúp na ďalšej" as an alert is the killer feature. Countdowns tick on the device, so the backend only pushes step changes and delays.
