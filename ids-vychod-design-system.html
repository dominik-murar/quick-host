Mobilná aplikácia IDS Východ · design system

Doprava bez hraníc,
native on every screen.

A design-system sketch for the IDS Východ passenger app (iOS, Android and Huawei AppGallery), the web journey planner on idsvychod.sk and the admin at admin.idsvychod.sk. It's built on the official Logomanuál IDS Východ (Príloha č. 3) and the functional specification Príloha č. 1 – Špecifikácia Mobilnej aplikácie.

R63823 701Os 8813N1

Concept work. The logo and brand colours come from the public tender documents. Line numbers, operators, fares and connection IDs are illustrative.

What changed from v0.1 after reading the logo manual and the specification

  • Brand: the invented "Tatra Dawn" palette is replaced by the heraldic red #FF0000 and blue #0066B3, the official logo and pin symbol, and Lato as the single typeface.
  • Navigation: the main screen now has the five required sections (Vyhľadať spojenie, Cestovné lístky, Zastávky a linky, Mapa, Hodnotenie dopravy) plus a "Ďalšie" menu.
  • Network switch: "Spoje IDS Východ" ↔ "Spoje všetkých dopráv" is a global control reachable from the main screen.
  • Tickets: registered users only, paid from the in-app credit wallet (topped up through the bank gateway). A single ticket is tied to one searched route, allows max 2 transfers, and activates 5 min before departure. The QR code is generated on the server, and drivers without a reader check a visual "časová zámka" (time lock). The Apple Pay / Google Pay checkout and Wallet pass are gone.
  • New areas: the Hodnotenie dopravy rating module, the admin tables, Huawei builds without Google services, and SK + EN as the required languages. Search is server-side, so it works online only.
01 · Brand in product

The logo is fixed. The product borrows its pieces.

The manual is binding ("záväzný predpis"). The logo may not be recoloured, reshaped or re-typeset, it needs a 5× clear zone, and the full lock-up has a minimum width of 25 mm. The product doesn't redraw the logo. It reuses three ideas from it.

① Pin "špendlík na mape"

The manual derives a standalone pin symbol for digital use (p. 11). It becomes the app icon, the splash mark, the destination marker on the map and the empty-state illustration.

② Parallel tracks

The red double line is the manual's "symbol dráhy". In the product it draws the journey connector, the ticket's time lock and the section dividers. The line is always a pair, never one stroke.

Námestie osloboditeľov
Staničné námestie

③ Arrow heading east

"The track has no start and no end", and it points right, toward the east. Forward motion in the UI goes left → right: moving lines on the time lock, the progress of a ride and the "next" affordances. Nothing animates westward.

Do
Put the full logo on the splash screen, the web header, the admin login and PDF tickets.
Don't
Squeeze the full lock-up into a 44 pt nav bar. Use the pin or the wordmark-free symbol there.

Proposed digital minimums (the manual gives only print sizes): full logo ≥ 120 px wide, symbol ≥ 32 px, pin ≥ 16 px. The clear zone is 5 grid units in every case. These need sign-off from the logo's author (the spec requires cooperating with them).

The manual allows "only red, blue and black" in the logo. The UI needs more than three colours, so it adds a derived layer: shades of the brand pair chosen to pass WCAG, plus fixed mode and status colours. The pure brand colours stay for identity moments.

The spec lists line types (električka, autobus, trolejbus…), and the manual recalls that red used to mean city transport and blue meant suburban buses. Shape and motion are owned by the platform

Shape tokens are semantic (shape.card, shape.sheet) and each platform resolves them its own way: iOS concentric corners and Liquid Glass bars, the Material 3 shape scale on Android, 8–12 px radii on the web. Vehicles glide between GPS updates instead of jumping. The time-lock animation and the vehicle easing turn off with Reduce Motion, and drivers then see the seconds clock and the colour of the day instead.

Rules

  • Live data is shown with a green colour, a pulsing dot and the word "naživo". Without live data, show "podľa CP" (per timetable). Live data exists only for operators that share it (§2.1), so "podľa CP" is a normal state, not an error.

Ticket lifecycle and the "časová zámka"

◔ Platba prebieha…Kúpený · aktivuje sa o 4:12● Platný · 58:12Expirovaný

  • Payment in progress (§2.2.1): the Buy button locks and shows a spinner, and a banner on the Lístky tab stays visible until the bank confirms. This prevents double purchases.
  • Activation starts automatically 5 min before the first connection leaves the boarding stop. The countdown is shown before that.
  • Every ticket lists zones, validity, each leg with its times, and the line "Lístok platí aj na iné spoje v rámci zónovej a časovej platnosti".

Proposal: visual check for drivers without a QR reader (§2.2.2)

The spec asks the supplier to propose this mechanism. The time-lock band combines four signals that a screenshot can't fake:

  1. Moving tracks: the red double line from the logo with an arrow running east.
  2. A clock with seconds that matches the driver's own clock.
  3. Colour and letter of the day, set in the admin and pushed daily, so yesterday's screenshot is visibly wrong.
  4. Tap to react: tapping the band inverts it with a haptic tick, so the driver can ask the passenger to tap.

06 · Architecture (spec §1–§5)

Where the design system lives

The spec defines four separate modules. Everything connects to Jadro, and clients talk to the search engine only through Jadro's API. The design system lives entirely on the client side. One token source feeds four surfaces, and future ones (dispatch, passenger displays) can plug in too.

📱 Užívateľská aplikácia

  • iOS · SwiftUI
  • Android · Compose (GMS flavour)
  • Android · Compose (HMS flavour, AppGallery)

🌐 Webový vyhľadávač

  • idsvychod.sk · same functions as the app search (§3.2)

🛠 Webový administrátor

  • admin.idsvychod.sk · tables, reports, heat maps
↔ only through the Jadro API

Modul „Jadro“ (backend)

  • API: search · timetables · users · messages
  • Database: configuration, payments, timetables, ratings
  • Platobná brána: one bank, credit top-up; direct purchase later
  • E-wallets: managed by IDS Východ
  • Server-side QR: type, zones/time, number, user ID, rotating code, purchase time, timestamp
  • Routing to stops (Google or another provider) through a backend cache

Vyhľadávač spojení

  • online, 2500 req/s, < 1 s
  • two networks: IDS / all
  • hubs, guaranteed transfers

Hodnotenie dopravy

  • trip and stop rating
  • configurable criteria
  • heat maps, CSV export

Pracovný server

  • import via FTP over OpenVPN: TXT, JDF, XML, CSV
  • validate → one transactional push → production

Dopravcovia

  • timetables
  • live delays and GPS (when an operator shares them)

Later

  • Central IDS dispatch
  • Passenger information displays → the same mode tokens on signage

What this means for design: the clients stay thin. Fare rules, ticket eligibility and QR content come from the server, so the UI only shows server decisions (eligibility chips, activation time) and never computes them. Because the spec requires native apps, each platform builds its components natively. Only tokens, icons, copy and specs are shared.

08 · Accessibility

Non-negotiables

Perceive

  • WCAG 2.2 AA. Pure #FF0000 is never used for text.
  • Mode shown as colour + shape + glyph.
  • Live status shown as colour + dot + word.
  • Every map view has a list equivalent.

Operate

  • Lato scales with Dynamic Type / sp up to 200 % without cutting off times.
  • Full VoiceOver, TalkBack and keyboard paths, including the time lock (announced as "Lístok platný, zostáva 58 minút").

Understand

  • SK and EN required. Stop names are never translated.
  • Ticket rules in plain language on the ticket itself.
  • Layouts tested with EN strings 40 % longer.
09 · Tokens & open questions

One source, native outputs

Tokens are written in the W3C Design Tokens format and compiled with Style Dictionary into Swift, Compose and CSS. There are three tiers: brand (logo manual values, locked), semantic (modes, status, surfaces, light and dark) and platform (mapped onto Material roles and iOS semantic colours). Components only use semantic and platform tokens.

Data-viz for the admin's rating charts and heat maps: a sequential blue ramp built from #0066B3 for counts, and a diverging red↔blue ramp for satisfaction. That reuses both heraldic colours and stays readable for colour-blind users.

Open questions

  • For the logo's author: confirm the screen values (#FF0000/#0066B3 vs. the artwork's #EB2C2E/#0072B7 and RGB 10/81/161), digital minimum sizes, and whether red can be used as a UI accent (time lock, alert dot).
  • Mode colours vs. vehicle liveries and signage at DPMK, DPMP, SAD and ZSSK. Does IDS want its own palette or the operators' colours?
  • Time-lock mechanism: agree with IDS and the operators (§2.2.2). Who sets the colour and letter of the day, and when does it change?
  • Payment gateway: does the chosen bank support Apple Pay / Google Pay for top-ups? A card-only top-up flow is designed as the fallback.
  • Languages: the spec requires SK + EN. Should HU or UK be planned for the southern districts and refugees?
  • PDF ticket (§4.6 Účet): use the same layout as the in-app ticket, with the full logo and the double-track divider.
