Spoj · colour proposal · 30 Oct 2026

One hue, one meaning.

Keep Ember's warm paper, aubergine ink and warm-charcoal nights. Give it back a chromatic tint, and keep that tint Ubian green. Then give every other hue exactly one job: red for things that went wrong, amber for delays, and three mode colours that never look like a status.

Short answer

  • Ember's ink-as-primary doesn't carry over to Liquid Glass. Glass chrome is already monochrome: every glyph on a toolbar is label-coloured. A black primary is only a heavier version of its neighbours, and at night a white glass-prominent button becomes the brightest thing on a dark map. On iOS, the tint is the one place a brand can show inside system chrome. A monochrome tint gives that place away.
  • On Material 3 it's legitimate. SchemeMonochrome is a first-class variant, and a filled button with an on-primary label works. M3 Expressive pushes the other way, though, towards more colour. The same tokens should work on both platforms, so I'd drop ink-as-primary on both.
  • Ember's character isn't in its accent anyway. It comes from the cream, the aubergine ink and the warm map. On iOS those show in the content layer: the map, the sheet's opaque body, cards. The glass picks them up from what's underneath it.
  • Ember also silently dropped a status. In Spoj, accent paints the live and on-time times, the countdowns and the done ticks. Make the accent ink and "live" turns into plain text. Tint and live need their own roles.
  • Mode colours today reuse status colours. Klasická's train colour can't be told from its delay colour (ΔE 2.2), its city lines are the "missed" red, and its regional buses are the exact info blue.

Mode colours

Red, amber and green are taken by status, so modes take the cool half of the wheel and a red-violet. Light mode spaces them by lightness as well as hue, so they stay apart for colour-blind riders. The badge shapes from the manual still carry the mode on their own.

A bolder alternative: Ember Plum

Push Ember's aubergine ink up to full chroma and make that the tint. Green is then left meaning only "live", and purple primaries are rare on iOS, so it would be unmistakably Spoj.

What it costs: it drops Ubian's green, the brand the manual pitches. MHD would have to leave plum for cobalt, and regional would go to petrol, which sits 11 from live green. "Go" stops being the brand's colour. I'd keep it as a theme, not the default.

What it means for the code

  • Split accent into tint (interactive) and live (status), even while both are green. Today a single key does both jobs, and that's how Ember lost "live".
  • Delete info: the modes menu ticks and the layers dot become tint, and notices become a neutral surface with an icon.
  • Delete ride, rideRoute and routeStop: a ride, its timeline and its route take their leg's mode colour, as the trip strip already does.
  • Collapse the neutrals. 51 neutral keys per scheme, 13 of them text greys, become the platform ladder of about 10. palette.ts shrinks by more than half.
  • The delay pill everywhere, so delayPill stops being nullable.
  • Native later: on iOS, text on glass uses PlatformColor('label') and friends, so it stays vibrant as the glass adapts, and the tint is AccentColor. On Android, build the scheme with material-color-utilities: Ubian green as the primary seed and Ember's warm neutral palette. Modes and delay are harmonised custom colours. Material You's wallpaper colours can be an Android-only theme choice, applied to tint and neutrals and never to modes or status.
  • Themes: one default (Ember neutrals with the green tint), Plum as an option, and Neutrálna and Medená retired as finished experiments.
