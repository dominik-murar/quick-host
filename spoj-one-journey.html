One journey, not a planner and a Jazda

How the trip planner's journey details and the live Jazda view should relate, what other transit apps do, and a phased plan that also brings multi-leg journeys to Jazda. Based on main at 65d77ba.

Merge them. Journey details and Jazda show the same thing, a connection, at different moments. Other apps keep one route object and one layout. Riding adds a "now" header and moves the focus along the route. It never switches you to a second view.

Keeping a journey and being guided through it are separate. You skip tracking because tracking is a mode you have to switch on. Make keeping implicit and cheap: a journey you opened stays as Vaša cesta until it ends, with live delays and transfer waits. Turn guidance (instructions, get-off cue, handover at transfers) on automatically once isOnBoard detects you on the vehicle. That retires the Sledovať toggle.

Multi-leg comes free. Store the whole connection instead of TrackedRide with nextLegs. Jazda then becomes the journey timeline in live mode, and transfer stops get the leg alternatives and departures panels you already built.

Patterns that recur

  1. One route object, one layout. Planning and riding are densities of the same screen, never two screens.
  2. Keeping is low-commitment. Rail apps lead here. Commuters who know the route still want the delay, so saving a journey is separate from step-by-step guidance and costs nothing.
  3. The transfer is the headline. At a change, "will I make it" matters more than where the vehicle is. The wait left and the risk get top billing.
  4. Recovery stays in place. When a change slips, the alternatives appear at that step, not in a new search.
  5. The compact form shows the next event (board, get off, change), not the current leg's name.

The three highlighted boxes are one component fed by one KeptJourney { connection, keptAt }. What changes between them is density and focus:

  • Planning: legs folded, header "Odchod o 6 min", alternatives swipeable. This is today's JourneyDetails.
  • Kept: the pill on the map shows the next event across all legs, with delay. Tapping it opens the same timeline, focused on now.
  • Jazda: past legs collapse to one line, the current leg unfolds with the vehicle on the rail, and the next transfer shows its live wait. Later legs stay folded with their known delay.
  • Single trip from Odchody becomes a one-leg connection, so ConnectionDetails goes away.

Decisions for you

  1. Implicit keep or an explicit "Uložiť cestu"? I recommend implicit, limited to journeys within about 30 min and dropped with ✕, because your habit is exactly the case where an explicit step gets skipped. The risk is surprise: a pill you didn't ask for. If that bothers you, the fallback is a one-tap "Pripnúť" in place of Sledovať.
  2. Drop Sledovať entirely? I recommend yes, with guidance following isOnBoard. Keep a manual "Som vo vozidle" only if GPS detection proves unreliable on trains, which report only at stations.
  3. One kept journey or a list? Rail apps keep a list. I recommend one for now. The pill has room for one, and the model can grow into a list later.
  4. Does Jazda stay a separate overlay? The pill-to-sheet morph is a good signature, so keep it as the way in. The surface it grows into should be the journey's own page, though, with no second copy alongside it in the sheet.
